# Rubik's Cube Resources

## Knowledge

- [J Perm: How To Solve The 3×3 Rubik's Cube](https://jperm.net/3x3)
  Step-by-step beginner method with photos. Minimal notation required. Use for: the primary solve path in this workspace.
- [J Perm: Beginner Tutorial (YouTube, ~10 min)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7Ron6MN45LY)
  Video walkthrough of the same layer-by-layer method. Use for: watching hand movements and timing.
- [CubeSkills: The Beginner's Method](https://www.cubeskills.com/tutorials/the-beginners-method-for-solving-the-rubiks-cube)
  Structured 13-lesson course by former world champion Feliks Zemdegs. Use for: formal notation and cube structure when you want deeper grounding.
- [J Perm: Beginner Method Example Solve](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LS2GqHQ9-_g)
  Full solve demonstration. Use for: seeing how all seven steps connect after you've learned them individually.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/Cubers](https://www.reddit.com/r/Cubers/)
  Friendly beginner-friendly community. Use for: troubleshooting stuck steps, cube recommendations, encouragement.
- [SpeedSolving.com Forums](https://www.speedsolving.com/forum/)
  Long-running cubing forum. Use for: method comparisons and detailed troubleshooting once basics are solid.
- [World Cube Association (WCA)](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/)
  Official competitions and records. Use for: finding local meetups when you're ready to test skills in the real world.
