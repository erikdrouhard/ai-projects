Lesson 1: Build the White Cross

Rubik's Cube · Beginner Method · ~10 min with a cube in hand

Your win today: Place all four white edge pieces around the white center so you have a white cross on top — and each edge's second color matches the center on that side.

This lesson is step 1 of the J Perm beginner method. Every full solve starts here. Read Cube Anatomy first if piece names are unfamiliar.

Before you start

  • Hold the cube with the white center on top.
  • You're looking for edge pieces — they have exactly two colors, one of which is white.
  • Ignore corners for now. Corners come in Lesson 2.

The algorithm (in plain English)

For each white edge stuck in the bottom layer:

  1. Find a white edge in the bottom layer.
  2. Look at its other color (e.g. red). Turn the bottom layer until that edge sits directly under the center of the same color.
  3. Turn that side face once to lift the edge to the top.
  4. Check: white sticker on top, side color matches the side center.
Edge flipped wrong? If white faces the side instead of up, do: bottom turn to align → lift → bottom turn back → lift again. See J Perm's flipped-edge photos.
Edge stuck in the middle? Move it down to the bottom first with: turn the side it's on once, turn bottom once, turn that side back. Then solve it normally.

Hands-on practice

Scramble your cube (or use a simulator).

Build the white cross. Time yourself — aim for under 3 minutes by your third attempt.

When done, all four side colors on the cross edges must match their center stickers.

Done? What's next

Lesson 2 will use the "4-move sequence" to snap white corners into place and complete the entire first layer. Don't rush — a clean cross makes everything after it easier.
