Cube Anatomy

Quick reference — 3×3 Rubik's Cube piece types

A standard 3×3 cube has 26 visible pieces (the core is hidden). Every solve method builds on knowing which piece is which.

Three piece types

Center — 1 color, never moves relative to other centers.
There are 6 centers (white, yellow, green, blue, red, orange). They define each face's color.
Edge — 2 colors, sits between two centers.
There are 12 edge pieces. Example: white–red edge has white on one side and red on the other.
Corner — 3 colors, sits where three faces meet.
There are 8 corner pieces. Example: white–red–green corner touches white, red, and green faces.

Key insight

Centers never swap places — only edges and corners move. When someone says "solve the white face," they mean align edges and corners around the white center, not move the center itself.

Beginner method anchor

  • Step 1: White cross — 4 white edges around white center
  • Step 2: First layer — 4 white corners
  • Step 3: Second layer — 4 middle edges
  • Steps 4–7: Last layer (cross, align, corners, finish)
