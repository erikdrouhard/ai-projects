Move Notation

Standard face-turn notation for 3×3 cubes

Each letter names a face. A plain letter turns that face clockwise as if you're looking directly at it. A prime (') means counter-clockwise. A 2 means turn twice (180°).

Face letters

LetterFaceWhen white is on top
UUp (top)White face
DDown (bottom)Yellow face
FFrontFace toward you
BBackFace away from you
RRightRight side
