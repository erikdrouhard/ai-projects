# Mission: Solve the Rubik's Cube

## Why
Solve a scrambled 3×3 Rubik's Cube reliably from memory — without looking up steps mid-solve — so it stops feeling like an impossible puzzle and becomes a repeatable skill you can show off or use to unwind.

## Success looks like
- Complete a full solve using the beginner layer-by-layer method
- Reproduce the solve on a freshly scrambled cube without tutorial help
- Finish a solve in under 5 minutes with consistent technique

## Constraints
- Start from zero assumed experience (adjust if you already know more)
- Use the beginner method first — no speedcubing shortcuts until the full solve is solid
- Practice with a physical cube when possible; a simulator works for learning moves

## Out of scope
- CFOP / speedcubing optimization (for now)
- Blindfolded, one-handed, or bigger cubes (4×4, 5×5)
- Memorizing dozens of algorithms before the first full solve
